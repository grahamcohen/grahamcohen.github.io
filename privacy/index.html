Graham Cohen Analytics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Graham Cohen Analytics is a personal tool used only by Graham Cohen to read performance statistics (such as views, reach, likes, shares, and watch time) for his own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and is not used with anyone else’s account.

What data the app accesses

The app reads basic profile information, the list of posts and reels, and the insights Instagram provides for Graham Cohen’s own professional account. It does not read messages, does not post or change anything on Instagram, and does not access data about other people’s accounts.

How the data is used and stored

The data is saved only on Graham Cohen’s personal computer for his own analysis. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

To remove the app’s access, open Instagram, go to Settings → Apps and websites, and remove Graham Cohen Analytics. Data the app has already saved is stored only on the developer’s own computer and can be deleted there at any time.
